Exam logistics

Registration, retakes, and results

Testing windows

Three windows per year (typically Oct–Nov, Jan–Feb, Apr–May, Philippine time) with limited slots per session — register early for your preferred date.

Retakes & validity

Retakes are allowed, but only one registration per testing cycle (duplicate payments are forfeited). Scores are valid for admission within two years (CHED CMO 18, s. 2016).

Qualifying score

The qualifying percentile rank is the higher of the school's own cut-off or the CHED 40th-percentile minimum — check each program's published PR.

Fees & results

Domestic fee is about ₱1,900 (international USD 60) via the official payment channels. Electronic results arrive in about 20 CEM business days; the printed report follows later. Scores go to your chosen schools via the official masterlist (a waiver option exists).
